來源摘要（原文）
MIT researchers have developed a method that lets AI climate models get by with a single training run instead of the usual six to eight. An algorithm generates deliberately unrealistic scenarios with extreme CO2 or methane swings that make the model more robust. Until now, these emulators were often outdated by the time they were finished, according to the researchers.
