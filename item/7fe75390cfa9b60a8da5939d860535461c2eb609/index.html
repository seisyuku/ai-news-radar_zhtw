Summary
Tencent's Gander combines real-time conversation with AI agent capabilities. A "cerebellum" handles the conversation while a swappable "brain" tackles complex tasks in the background. Users can interrupt at any time. In benchmark tests, Gander interrupted users in just 8 percent of cases, though it still trailed competitors on task accuracy.
