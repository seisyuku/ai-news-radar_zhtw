Eight countries along with representatives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, and Mistral AI have signed the "Paris Call for AI Safety and Security" at Emmanuel Macron's initiative. The companies commit to shutdown mechanisms, independent audits, and disclosing known risks, but the agreement lacks binding sanctions or deadlines.
