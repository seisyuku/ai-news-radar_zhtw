Title
OpenAI reopens its $200 Pro plan but cuts API credits in half as it nudges users toward pay-per-use
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-29T10:05:14Z
Summary
OpenAI is reopening its $200 Pro subscription to new sign-ups but cutting API credits per dollar in half. More efficient models like GPT-6 Sol and Luna make up for the difference, employee Thibault Sottiaux says. The move is part of OpenAI's shift from subsidized flat-rate plans toward usage-based billing.
