Title
Meta and Microsoft pull back from Claude as Anthropic transforms from partner into competitor
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-10-05T18:59:19Z
Summary
Meta and Microsoft, two of Anthropic's biggest enterprise customers, are sharply cutting their use of Claude. Microsoft slashed the monthly per-employee budget in its cloud division from $100,000 to $10,000, while Meta halved its Claude Code users to 30,000. Both companies are pushing their own AI tools instead. For Anthropic, that reliance on a few major clients is turning into a strategic risk.
