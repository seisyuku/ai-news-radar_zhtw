來源摘要（原文）
Nvidia's new AI model GR00T 2 is designed to teach robots new tasks without laborious teleoperation. Instead, the system transfers human hand movements from videos to robotic arms, trained on about 400,000 hours of video footage. Success rates still vary widely, though, ranging from 10 to 80 percent depending on the task.
