來源摘要（原文）
Since November 26, DeepSeek has been gray-scale testing on the web and in the App a display of the current round's consumption quota: after completing a conversation, a details pop-up appears, showing the number of tokens and estimated amount for the round, with prices in line with the API. Personal chats remain free, but the cost of a single calculation has become visible; the quota is estimated based on off-peak pricing, and a screenshot shows that a 0.1-yuan bill was split into three charges.
