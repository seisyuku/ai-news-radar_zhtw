Australia's government, led by Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, wants to enshrine mandatory safety rules for frontier AI companies in law for the first time. A new regulator would enforce independent model audits and impose steep penalties for violations. With this move, Canberra is deliberately prioritizing domestic regulation before entering international talks.
