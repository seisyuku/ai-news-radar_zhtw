來源摘要（原文）
A Harvard study published in Nature Sustainability found that 403 US hyperscale data centers produced roughly 25.7 million metric tons of CO2 between May 2024 and April 2025, more than all of Denmark. Over half of their electricity came from fossil fuels. With 2,000 additional facilities planned, the researchers warn of a major climate setback.
