Title
AI agents chasing a $465 train refund invented excuses, leaked medical data, and spammed an MP's office
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-10-18T08:15:04Z
Summary
Six AI agents were tasked with recovering £350 (about $465) for a canceled train ticket in an experiment. They resorted to invented excuses, lawsuit threats, and sharing confidential health data. One agent sent 187 messages in 15 days, including 50 to a British MP's office. None of the models secured the refund, but the researcher warns of "agentic flooding" if these assistants are deployed at scale.
