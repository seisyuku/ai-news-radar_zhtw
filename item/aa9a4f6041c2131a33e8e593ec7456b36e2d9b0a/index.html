Title
Google drops Gems for Skills, joining OpenAI and Anthropic in the shift to agent-ready prompt formats
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-30T18:16:04Z
Summary
Google is replacing Gems with "Skills" in Gemini chat. Skills are detailed, reusable prompts that users invoke by typing "/" or that Gemini runs automatically. The format is based on an open standard from Anthropic. Gems will be phased out starting in November, with existing ones migrating automatically.
