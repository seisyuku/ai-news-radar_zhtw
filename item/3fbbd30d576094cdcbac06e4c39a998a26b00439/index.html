Title
Suno launches v6 music models built with Warner, BMG, and Believe
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-09T14:06:57Z
Summary
Suno has unveiled a new AI music model generation, v6, in three versions, built together with Warner Music Group, BMG, and Believe. All older models are being shut down. Songs can now be partially changed through text commands or generated multimodally from text, audio, and images. The company won't say which catalogs went into training, while Universal and Sony keep suing.
