OpenAI has significantly strengthened safeguards against extracting internal reasoning from GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. In adaptive red-teaming tests, the success rate for extracting protected reasoning fell from 54 percent to 0.2 percent. The company is also suspending thousands to tens of thousands of accounts each week over coordinated distillation campaigns.
