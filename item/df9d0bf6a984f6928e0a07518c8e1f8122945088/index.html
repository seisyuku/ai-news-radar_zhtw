OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has released internal perplexity scores for the entire GPT series for the first time, up to an unreleased model beyond the current generation. The metric shows that core capabilities emerge at surprisingly precise thresholds. Pachocki is pushing to make perplexity an industry-wide progress metric and establish shared limits before control over AI behavior erodes.
