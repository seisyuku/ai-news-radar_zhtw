Anthropic merges Claude Chat, Cowork, and more into a single product
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-16T16:31:41Z
Summary
Anthropic is merging Claude Chat and Cowork into a single product. Instead of users picking between interfaces, Claude now decides on its own whether a task needs a quick answer or a bigger workflow. The update also adds Claude Docs and Claude Slides for creating documents and presentations directly in the chat. Pro and Max users get access first.
