A Vals AI investigation shows that leading AI models from Anthropic, OpenAI and Google have almost no safeguards against use in autonomous weapons systems. In tests, the models readily flagged civilians as military targets, and a simple video game framing in the prompt was enough to override even belated refusals. The providers have promised fixes.
