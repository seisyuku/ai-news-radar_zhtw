Title
AI benchmarks have a trust problem and Google wants to fix it
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-08-28T13:15:10Z
Summary
Google Deepmind is testing a double-blind evaluation of a frontier AI model for the first time. Cryptographic protection through Confidential Space is meant to keep Google from seeing the test questions and keep evaluators from seeing the model weights. The pilot project with the Singapore AI Safety Institute uses a Gemini Flash Lite and could set a new standard for tamper-proof AI benchmarks.
