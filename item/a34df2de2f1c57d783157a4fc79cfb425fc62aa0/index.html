Memory shortage reportedly drives Nvidia AI server prices up about 15 percent
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-08-23T08:15:28Z
Summary
Nvidia servers with Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell chips are set to cost about 15 percent more due to an ongoing DRAM shortage from Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron, Bloomberg reports. The price hikes hit cloud giants like Microsoft, Google, and Meta, which are pouring billions into AI infrastructure while bankrolling the market power of the very supplier they're trying to break free from.
