Title
OpenAI acquires Orchestra Bio to build AI agents that speed up drug development
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-10-21T08:32:54Z
Summary
OpenAI is acquiring startup Orchestra Bio and plans to merge it with its life sciences AI platform Rosalind. The goal is to build AI agents that help pharma teams coordinate drug development, from tracking trials to spotting problems. Developing a new drug in the US takes ten to 15 years on average. OpenAI already works with Novo Nordisk and Moderna on AI-driven pharma research.
