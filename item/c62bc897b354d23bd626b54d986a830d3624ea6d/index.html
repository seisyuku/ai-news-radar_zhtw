GPT‑6.2 Astra and GPT‑6.2 Sol OpenAI: We’re introducing GPT‑6.2 Astra, our new frontier model for your hardest projects, and GPT‑6.2 Sol, our efficient, versatile model for everyday work. They're available in the API as gpt-6.2-astra and gpt-6.2-sol . I shipped LLM 0.38 with support for the new models. 6.2 Astra is 80% of the price of 6 Astra - $8/MTok input and $40/MTok output, the same price as Claude Fable 5.2 (which was also 80% of its predecessor). 6 Astra and Fable 5 were both $10/$50. The cached price is an even bigger discount - $0.40/million compared to $1/million for the older model. This is great news for longer running agentic sessions.
