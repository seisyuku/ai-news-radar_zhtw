來源摘要（原文）
Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini, an open looped transformer that runs 12 of its 24 layers three times per token. This lets the model reason internally for longer without generating extra text. On Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, it ties with Qwen3.8 27B at roughly six times lower cost.
