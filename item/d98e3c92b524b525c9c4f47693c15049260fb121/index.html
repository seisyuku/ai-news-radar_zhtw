Title
A Claude model autonomously chose a gene therapy and operated on a mouse brain with no human decision-making
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-10-30T19:42:59Z
Summary
An AI agent performed brain surgery on an epileptic mouse, picking the drug, the brain target, and the injection coordinates on its own. According to lead researcher Ocana-Santero, it's the first time an AI agent has autonomously chosen a treatment and delivered it to a living mammal. But when a calibration error hit mid-surgery, the agent refused to continue and couldn't be talked out of it.
