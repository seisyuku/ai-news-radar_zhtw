Title
IBM drops open-weight Granite 4.2 family with built-in agentic capabilities under Apache 2.0
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-08-26T10:37:56Z
Summary
IBM is releasing its Granite 4.2 language models in 3B, 8B, and 30B sizes, trained on about 15 trillion tokens with a context window of up to 512,000 tokens. The larger models use "agentic RL" training to learn tool use and code execution on their own. All models are available under the Apache 2.0 license.
