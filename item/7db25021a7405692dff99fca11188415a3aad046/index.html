來源摘要（原文）
Terence Tao outlines his vision of "Big Math" on the Hard Fork podcast. Instead of solo work, human-AI teams would systematically map thousands of problems. He also calls AI proofs from major tech companies "toxic waste," saying the models solve problems but can't explain their results. Tao hopes for a new division of labor where AI delivers the first rough solution and humans put it in context.
