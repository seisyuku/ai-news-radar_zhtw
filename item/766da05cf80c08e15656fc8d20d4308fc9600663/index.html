來源摘要（原文）
KPMG deployed more than 100 AI agents alongside the regular audit of a UK-listed company. The agents asked "better questions" than the audit team in some cases, according to UK head of audit Cath Burnet. The UK's accounting regulator, the FRC, sees a risk in the approach because AI agents analyze far more data than traditional sampling methods, potentially surfacing findings that could serve as a sales pitch for lucrative consulting work and undermine auditor independence.
