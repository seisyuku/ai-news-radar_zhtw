Title
Reddit's r/opensource bans all AI-related projects, sparking backlash and a rival subreddit
Source

Published

Summary
The Reddit forum r/opensource has banned all posts about AI projects, whether built with AI, AI-powered, or designed for AI. Projects must also be at least a year old and have multiple contributors, and non-English posts are excluded as well. In response, the rival forum r/TrueOpenSource has already popped up.
