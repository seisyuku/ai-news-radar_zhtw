Google Deepmind's new Gemini Decisions API evaluates up to 1,000 closed-ended questions in a single pass across text, images, audio, and video. Gemini Decision 1 returns results in 150 to 250 milliseconds at roughly five times lower cost than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, according to Google.
