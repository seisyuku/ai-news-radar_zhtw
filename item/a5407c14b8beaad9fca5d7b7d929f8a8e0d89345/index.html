A new real-world test puts AI coding agents in a simulated developer job with colleagues, project boards, and incomplete handoffs, where they have to keep developing software on their own and reliably close open tickets in a messy daily workflow. Even Opus 5.5 and Astra fall far short, solving fewer than one in ten tasks completely. The models finish clear, standalone assignments quickly, but they lose track as autonomous developers.
