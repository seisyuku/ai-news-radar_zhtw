Major US banks are locking out AI agents that log in with customer credentials
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published

Summary
Customers are letting AI agents use their credentials to access accounts at Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, and other major U.S. banks, sometimes without direct instructions.
