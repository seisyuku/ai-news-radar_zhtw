Title
China's AI industry closes ranks as Deepseek ships open-source software for Huawei's Ascend chips
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-30T14:37:24Z
Summary
Deepseek and Huawei have built open-source programming tools for Huawei's Ascend AI chips. At the center is TileLang, a language designed to offer a simpler programming model than Nvidia's CUDA. The partnership targets the biggest obstacle for China's AI industry, which is software that gets the most out of domestic chips.
