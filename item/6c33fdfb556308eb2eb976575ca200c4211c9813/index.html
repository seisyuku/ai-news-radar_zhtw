GPT 6.2 Astra Solves a Historical Mystery Here's an interesting case-study in using GPT-6.2 Astra for historical research, from history professor Mark Humphries: With only minimal prompting, GPT-6.2 Astra was able to autonomously identify the creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale’s Beinecke Library . It did so extremely quickly (in just 22 minutes), using publicly available primary and secondary sources. To do so, it discovered a little known source in a paper archive, predicted that it would prove its hypothesis if examined and, in the end, it did. Not only that, but this new source turns out to be a major find on its own that will be of significant interest to both Canadian and fur trade historians. Here's the digitized sketchbook . Astra found a diary reference reference to a visit by the vicomte de Chabot dated five days before a date mentioned in the sketchbook at a nearby river, and suggested that Chabot's journal of his travels (only available by order from Cleveland State University) might confirm that the sketchbook was his work.
