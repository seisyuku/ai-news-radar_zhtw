Title
Google's Suncatcher project aims to put AI data centers in orbit powered by solar energy
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-24T17:45:44Z
Summary
Google's "Suncatcher" project aims to run AI infrastructure in orbit on solar power. A fridge-sized experimental satellite is set to launch on a SpaceX Falcon 9 on October 1. But the challenges are steep: you'd need around 10,000 satellites to match a single 1-gigawatt data center on Earth, and Jeff Bezos thinks it could take 20 years before orbital data centers beat ground-based ones on cost.
