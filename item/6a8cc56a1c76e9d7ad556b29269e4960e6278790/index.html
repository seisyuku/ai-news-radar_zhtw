OpenAI's safety team systematically investigated why the chains of thought in frontier models like GPT-6 Astra are increasingly evading oversight. Four training steps drive the erosion, including architecture choices and reinforcement learning. The analysis shows that the loss of transparency is not a law of nature but also reflects tradeoffs around capability and cost.
