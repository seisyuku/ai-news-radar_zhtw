Title
OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 Astra to top-tier ChatGPT plans at half the rate of GPT-5.6 Sol
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-05T07:41:03Z
Summary
OpenAI has rolled out GPT-6 Astra to Pro, Enterprise, and Business Premium users, with Plus users expected to follow soon. Message allowances for the standard model are roughly half of what GPT-5.6 Sol offers: Plus users get an estimated 5 to 45 messages per five hours with Astra, versus 10 to 100 with Sol. Higher-tier subscribers also get GPT-6 Astra Pro with separate weekly caps. Free and Go users don't get access.
