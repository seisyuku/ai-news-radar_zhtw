OpenAI researcher Noam Brown is urging promising students to drop out, saying it's "not a great time to sit on the sidelines." The o1 co-creator says AI is still in a "centaur period" and struggles with planning and self-correction, though he expects a "very steep rate of progress" soon. According to Brown, language models are already being used to build the next generation of models.
