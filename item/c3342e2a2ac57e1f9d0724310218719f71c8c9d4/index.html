Title
Deepmind was built to chase AGI, but its new chief just wants Gemini 4 out the door
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-09-24T13:35:44Z
Summary
Google Deepmind chief Koray Kavukcuoglu wants to release Gemini 4 "much earlier" than the end of the year. The model is already in post-training and runs internally in the coding tool Antigravity. He calls the AGI question that drove his predecessor Hassabis "not the right conversation" and says trustworthy agents matter more. After Gemini 3.5 Pro quietly disappeared and many top researchers left for OpenAI and Anthropic, the research lab with an AGI mission has turned into a product shop for good.
