Release: llm-gemini 0.37 Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen today - the lowest cost member of the Gemini 4 family. It's actually not too far off the price of its much bigger siblings, Carbon and Argon. I'm finding Gemini 4's pricing really confusing. Here's the pricing scheme: Carbon and Argon are the same price , and both are scheduled to double in price on December 1st. If they're the same price, why have two different models? Google says that Argon "delivers Gemini 4 intelligence with faster responses and lower token spend per turn compared to Gemini 4 Carbon" - so it may be priced the same, but it will use less tokens per task. Hydrogen, supposedly the cheap model, is currently only a 25% discount on its more capable piers. The Hydrogen price will make more sense after December 1st, when it will represent a 75% discount. It's still considerably more expensive than Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 Luna - they're both $0.10/$0.50, so Hydrogen is 15x that. I had Hydrogen generate some pelicans .
