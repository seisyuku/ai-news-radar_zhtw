NVIDIA released Nemotron-Labs-Sigma, 14 open 3B and 8B continuous diffusion language model checkpoints. Sigma denoises text in a learned 16-D embedding space, distills to few-step samplers, and decodes up to 4.20x faster than autoregressive decoding.
