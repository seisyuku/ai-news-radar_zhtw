Title
Clearing chat history helps AI agents find better solutions
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-10-12T16:10:00Z
Summary
Microsoft researchers found that autonomous AI agents get trapped in what they call "idea basins" because of their growing chat context, local solution regions they can't escape. Their "Fork-and-Flush" method clears the chat history but keeps the workspace, achieving up to 66 percent better results on the same compute budget.
