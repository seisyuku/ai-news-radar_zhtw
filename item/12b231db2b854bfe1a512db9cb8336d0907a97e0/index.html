Title
ChatGPT now faces stricter EU oversight as a very large search engine
Source
The Decoder AI News
Published
2026-08-31T14:31:37Z
Summary
The EU Commission is classifying ChatGPT as a very large search engine under the Digital Services Act for the first time, with at least 45 million monthly EU users. By the end of 2026, OpenAI has to deliver risk assessments, transparency reports, and an ad archive, among other things. Whether the Commission can also demand access to training data is disputed among legal experts.
