來源摘要（原文）
Nvidia is close to acquiring AI startup Inception for at least $500 million, according to The Information. The company is just two years old and builds language models based on diffusion processes, an alternative approach to the token-by-token generation used by models like ChatGPT. The deal would bolster Nvidia's own open-source models and its push into robotics.
