來源摘要（原文）
Stanford researchers tested 50 widely cited claims about LLMs against 20 current models. 44 percent of the findings are already "expired," and another 34 percent have weakened significantly. The average lifespan is just 15.7 months. Claims about what models can't do expire fastest.
