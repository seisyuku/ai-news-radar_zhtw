Google Deepmind tests the reliability of 18 AI models with "Humanity's First Exam." Top models solve nearly every task family at least once, but even leaders like OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra stumble when asked for 16 straight correct answers. Visual perception accounts for 73 percent of errors, and more compute time doesn't fix the problem.
